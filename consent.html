Consent to Personal Data Processing

Effective date: July 28, 2026 · Privacy Policy · Cookies · Terms

By enabling the consent switch on sign-in (or otherwise expressly agreeing), you give Solf.ai (“Operator”) consent to process your personal data as described below and in the Privacy Policy.

1. Who you consent to

Operator of the Solf.ai service. Contact: Telegram @vwirex.

2. What data

  • account data from Google/VK sign-in (e.g. name, email, profile photo URL, provider id);
  • chat text, limited conversation context, and images you upload for analysis;
  • plan / usage data needed to provide the Service;
  • technical data (e.g. IP, timestamps) for security and operation;
  • payment-related status from YooKassa if you buy a paid plan (card numbers are processed by YooKassa).

3. Purposes

Providing the Service (chat, tools, account); authentication; limits and plans; payments; security / abuse prevention; responding to your requests; complying with law.

4. How data is processed

Collection, recording, storage, use, transfer (including to processors such as hosting, Google Gemini for AI replies, Google/VK for login, YooKassa for payments), and deletion — as needed for the purposes above. Details: Privacy Policy.

5. International processing

You acknowledge that processing may involve providers outside your country (including the United States), where required to operate the features you use (e.g. AI replies via Google Gemini, authentication, hosting).

6. Duration and withdrawal

Consent is valid while you use the Service and for the retention periods in the Privacy Policy, unless withdrawn earlier. You may withdraw consent by contacting @vwirex and/or deleting your account data request. Withdrawal does not affect processing already performed lawfully before withdrawal. Without this consent, account features that require personal data may be unavailable.
